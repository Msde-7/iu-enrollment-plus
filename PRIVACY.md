# Privacy Policy

IU Enrollment Plus does not collect, store or share any personal information.

The extension does not include or provide any grade data. A valid IU login is required, and each student downloads
the grade distributions from the IU Registrar through their own IU account.

## What the extension reads

On stellic.iu.edu the extension scans page text for course codes such as CSCI-C 211 and for instructor names, so it
can place grade badges beside them. Everything else on the page is ignored, and nothing it reads leaves your browser
except instructor names sent to RateMyProfessors as described below.

## Network requests

The extension makes two kinds of request.

When you click **Load grade data** on its welcome page, it downloads the grade distribution reports from the IU
Registrar at gradedistribution.registrar.indiana.edu, using the IU login already in your browser. It only asks for
access to that site when you click the button.

To show an instructor's rating it sends that instructor's name to RateMyProfessors at www.ratemyprofessors.com. No
information about you is included.

## Stored data

The extension saves your display settings with the browser's built in sync storage. It keeps the grade data it
downloaded and caches instructor ratings for up to a week in local storage. Removing the extension deletes all of it.
