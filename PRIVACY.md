# Privacy Policy

IU Stellic Plus does not collect, store or share any personal information.

## What the extension reads

On stellic.iu.edu the extension scans page text for course codes such as CSCI-C 211 and for instructor names, so it
can place grade badges beside them. Everything else on the page is ignored, and nothing it reads leaves your browser
except instructor names sent to RateMyProfessors as described below.

## Network requests

The extension makes one kind of request. To show an instructor's rating it sends that instructor's name to
RateMyProfessors at www.ratemyprofessors.com. No information about you is included.

Grade distributions ship inside the extension, so looking up a course makes no network request.

## Stored data

The extension saves your display settings with the browser's built in sync storage, and caches instructor ratings
locally for up to a week. Removing the extension deletes both.

## Contact
